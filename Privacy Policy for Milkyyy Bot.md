# Privacy Policy for Milkyyy Bot

**Effective Date:** 11 July 2026

Milkyyy Bot respects the privacy of its users.

## Information Collection

Milkyyy Bot does not intentionally collect, store, or share personal information or user data.

The bot may temporarily process information provided by Discord, such as message content or user identifiers, solely for the purpose of responding to commands and providing functionality. This information is not permanently stored.

## Data Storage

Milkyyy Bot does not maintain a database of user information, messages, server information, or usage history.

## Third-Party Services

Milkyyy Bot operates through Discord and is therefore subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

## Data Sharing

Milkyyy Bot does not sell, trade, or share user data with third parties.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of Milkyyy Bot following any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the owner or developer of Milkyyy Bot through Discord.
